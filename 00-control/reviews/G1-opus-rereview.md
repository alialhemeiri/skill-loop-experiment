## Ruling on the end-date convention question

**The premise is not supported by stored evidence. v0 does not answer the naive anniversary date. It answers null.**
Across cal-3 and cal-4 (the only batches matching the frozen fixtures), v0 produced exactly two non-null `contract_end_date` values — both CORRECT under day-before-anniversary (doc-10: start 2027-02-04 → 2028-02-03 ✓; doc-09: start 2027-01-18 → 2028-01-17 ✓). Deposit misses are likewise nulls, not wrong arithmetic (missed_present = 12 = exactly the 12 wrong fields). Every point of measured headroom is v0 DECLINING TO DERIVE, not deriving differently. Essentially 0pp rides on convention knowledge; ~7.5pp rides on whether the skill derives at all.
On the merits, the derivation is unambiguous: a twelve-month term commencing 2026-08-21 has last day 2027-08-20; the anniversary answer makes the term twelve months and one day. UAE/Ejari practice coincides with the arithmetic. No document leaks the gold end date in any rendered format (verified across all 16).
**Recommendation (a): fair as-is; register in FREEZE.md** — (b) stating the convention in-doc would remove the registered derivation requirement and ~75% of real headroom to fix an error mode never observed; (c) capping derived end dates would cut headroom to ~3.75pp, dangerously near the noise threshold. Over-concentration's fix is disclosure, not dilution.
FREEZE.md must record: (1) the rules and coverage (end = start + 1 year − 1 day, 16/16; deposit = rent // 20, 8/8 present; identical train/holdout); (2) the failure mode stated correctly — "v0 declines to derive and emits null", NOT "wrong convention"; (3) headroom composition (0.900; 9 end-date + 3 deposit nulls; ten fields at 10/10). Also: derivation load is structurally balanced — fields requiring the two rules: train 12.5%, holdout 12.5% exactly; with 50/50 null rates, the first review's MAJOR 7 confound is fully closed.
